# CN World-Class Design DNA

This demo intentionally avoids a generic care-home template.

## Framework rules adopted
- Research first; unsupported facts are omitted or marked.
- Two primary journeys: care customers / relatives + recruiting.
- Conversion first, contextual CTAs, mobile-first.
- SEO/GEO structure: entity, services, location, FAQ, job intent.
- No fake reviews, awards, staff, capacities or availability.
- Admin is a demo system, not proof of live data.

## Creative-dev patterns studied / translated
- darkroomengineering/lenis: smooth, low-friction scroll philosophy. Implementation stays lightweight and native in this draft.
- motiondivision/motion: restrained state transitions and spring-like micro-interaction philosophy.
- lukePeavey/SplitType: typographic reveal concept translated into lightweight word-level hero choreography.
- brunosimon/folio-2025: interaction must belong to the concept, not be decoration. CN signature is `HOME × 069 × Frankfurt`.
- Rive / Theatre.js principles: state-driven interaction and timeline discipline; not added as dependencies unless they improve a real production use case.

## Signature Experience
`ZU HAUSE. WEITER LEBEN.`

- Huge editorial home/lifestyle imagery.
- 069 as a Frankfurt identity device.
- Care funnel is embedded in the story, not a separate SaaS widget.
- Recruiting switches visual energy to acid lime and the `DU FEHLST.` statement.
- AI assistant is contextual and hands off to humans.

## Golden-master test
If CN's name/logo can be swapped for another care provider without redesigning the page, the design has failed.
